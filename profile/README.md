# CadCraft

Draft, model and build — with tools you already know.

**[Website](https://getcadcraft.com) | [Try the apps](https://getcadcraft.com/try) | [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download) | [Tutorials](https://getcadcraft.com/tutorials) | [Roadmap](https://getcadcraft.com/roadmap) | [News](https://getcadcraft.com/news)**

CadCraft is a family of eight open-source CAD and BIM apps for architects, engineers, drafters, estimators and detailers. Every app is free, reads and writes open formats, works on your own files, and can be driven by scripts and AI assistants.

## The apps

| App | What it does | Description | Get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**CadCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/cadcraft) | 2D drafting & documentation | Command-line drafting with layers, blocks, dimensions and layouts that plot. Opens and saves DWG and DXF. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/cadcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/cadcraft) |
| [**BimCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/bimcraft) | BIM authoring | Parametric walls, doors and windows on levels and grids, with views, schedules, sheets and clash checks from one model. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/bimcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/bimcraft) |
| [**ModelCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/modelcraft) | 3D modeling for design | Fast push/pull modeling with groups, components, guides and inference snapping, for massing and early design. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/modelcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/modelcraft) |
| [**MarkupCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/markupcraft) | Drawing review & markup | Open a PDF plan set, mark it up from a saved tool chest, compare revisions and run punch lists with the whole team. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/markupcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/markupcraft) |
| [**TakeoffCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/takeoffcraft) | Quantity takeoff & estimating | Calibrated linear, area and count takeoff from PDF plans, with assemblies, cost columns and quantities that export. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/takeoffcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/takeoffcraft) |
| [**CivilCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/civilcraft) | Civil & site design | Surfaces from points and breaklines, alignments, profiles, corridors, grading and pipe networks. LandXML in and out. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/civilcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/civilcraft) |
| [**DetailCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/detailcraft) | Steel & rebar detailing | Structural steel members and connections, rebar placement and bending schedules, shop drawings and NC files from one model. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/detailcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/detailcraft) |
| [**PartCraft**](https://github.com/getcadcraft/partcraft) | Parametric parts & assemblies | Constrained sketches, feature history, assemblies with joints and drawings with GD&T. STEP in and out. | [Try it](https://getcadcraft.com/try/partcraft) \| [Download](https://getcadcraft.com/download/partcraft) |

Version 0.4 of all eight runs in the browser and installs on Windows and Linux. macOS is coming in 0.5; iPad and Android tablets are planned.

Coming next: **SuperCadCraft**, the eight apps in one window, on the same files.

## Rules every app follows

- **Open formats first.** DWG, DXF, IFC, STEP, PDF and LandXML in and out. Your drawings never need us to open them.
- **Works offline.** Local files on your own disk, with no forced cloud. No sign-in and no license check to open a drawing or plot it.
- **Your muscle memory works.** The commands, aliases, layouts and shortcuts you already use do what your hands expect.
- **Scriptable to the command line.** A command-line interface, a JSON control channel and an MCP server, so scripts and AI assistants can draft, check and plot alongside you.
- **Open source.** Apache License 2.0. Read it, build it, change it.
